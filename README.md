# Hi there, I'm Denys 👋

I am a researcher bridging the gap between biological sciences and computational analysis. With a strong academic foundation in Veterinary Medicine and a Bachelor's degree in Biotechnology, I focus on applying data-driven approaches to solve complex biological problems.

## 🧬 Current Focus & Research Interests
- **Genetic Engineering & Synthetic Biology:** Exploring targeted gene editing, functional genomics, and the design of synthetic biological pathways to engineer novel traits and cellular functions.
- **Gerontology & Aging Biology:** Investigating the molecular mechanisms of aging, cellular senescence, and longevity to understand and potentially mitigate age-related physiological decline.
- **Bioinformatics & Genomics:** Developing custom Python and R scripts for DNA sequence analysis, mutation profiling, and variant calling.
- **Plant Biotechnology:** Researching callus culture induction, secondary metabolite synthesis, and stress-resistance mechanisms in medicinal and non-traditional plants.
- **Hardware & Engineering:** Designing and building custom laboratory equipment to optimize autonomous research environments.
